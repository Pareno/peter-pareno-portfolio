Who I Am

I'm a Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Specialist focused on securing operational technology in manufacturing environments. My work centers on protecting mission-critical CNC systems and complex OT/IT infrastructure, where security failures have real production consequences.

My background combines a Master's degree in Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and Information Technology. My Master's research examined access control vulnerabilities in SME manufacturing — a niche where security gaps often go unnoticed until they're exploited.

Over 5+ years leading IT operations in manufacturing, I've built security programs from the ground up, responded to real incidents, and helped teams move from reactive firefighting to resilient, standards-based defenses.
